# Turn Luvix into Vietnam's AI sales team

The fastest credible route to Luvix's first **1 billion VND** (about US$38–40k) is a barbell. On one side, the owner uses their C-suite network to sell **two or three paid enterprise pilots** worth 100–200M VND each, and converts at least one into an annual deployment. On the other, Luvix sells a Vietnamese-first, VND-priced AI content and sales-chatbot product to a few dozen SMEs on annual prepaid contracts, with paid setup. The barbell matters because Vietnamese AI projects typically run **1–3 billion VND each** ([Mekong ASEAN](https://mekongasean.vn/von-dau-tu-vao-doanh-nghiep-ai-viet-nam-tang-8-lan-47205.html)), while SME software sells for only a few hundred thousand VND per month ([fchat.vn](https://fchat.vn/price)). One enterprise logo can be worth as much as a hundred small customers. There is a serious caveat up front: **we do not know for certain what Luvix.ai is.** The only indexed luvix.ai site is a French-language, self-serve AI content writer priced in euros. It shows signs of being built on an off-the-shelf script, it has no named team, customers or B2B plan, and the owner has not confirmed it is their product ([luvix.ai](https://luvix.ai/); [luvix.ai/home/en](https://luvix.ai/home/en)). This plan therefore assumes the most likely product, an AI content and marketing assistant that extends into AI chatbots for sales and customer service. Section 6 explains what changes if that assumption is wrong. The long-term vision is to become the **Vietnamese-first "AI workforce" for marketing, sales and customer service**. In that market FPT already books hundreds of billions of VND in AI and cloud revenue ([FPT Annual Report 2025](https://fpt.com/en/news/fpt-annual-report-2025-ai-becomes-a-core-capability-in-growth-strategy)), which shows the money is real. It also shows Luvix must specialise rather than compete head-on.

## What luvix.ai looks like today, and why that is a starting point rather than a product

Every fact about Luvix in this report comes from search-engine snippets. Direct fetches of luvix.ai failed in the research environment, so **the owner should check every point against the live product.** The indexed site is titled "Luvix - Meilleur de générateur de contenu IA". It promises SEO-optimised blog, ad, email and website copy "10x" faster, offers **"60+" (elsewhere "72") writing templates** for Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, e-commerce and SEO, and includes an AI chat plus persona chatbots such as "business coach", "lawyer" and "doctor" ([luvix.ai](https://luvix.ai/); [luvix.ai/home/en](https://luvix.ai/home/en)). Pricing is individual and in euros: a 5-day trial, **€97/year** for 30,000 words a month, a **€199 lifetime** deal, and prepaid word packs from €5. There are no seats, teams, invoices or API ([luvix.ai/home/en](https://luvix.ai/home/en)). The only contact is hello@luvix.ai. The privacy policy names no legal entity ([luvix.ai privacy policy](https://luvix.ai/page/politiques-de-confidentialite)). The research found **no Vietnamese interface, no VND pricing, no named customers, no reviews and no social or press footprint**, and Vietnamese-language searches for Luvix returned only competitors ([VnExpress: LovinBot AI](https://vnexpress.net/lovinbot-ai-nen-tang-so-hoa-ai-cho-doanh-nghiep-4914035.html)).

Three features of the site point to a white-label "AI writer SaaS" script of the kind sold on CodeCanyon rather than proprietary technology: the template count, the membership-versus-prepaid plan tabs, and the `/page/<slug>` URL pattern. The pricing looks unrevised (the €5 pack gives more words than the yearly plan's monthly allowance). This is an inference, not a confirmed fact. If it is true, it has two consequences. First, Luvix has **no technical moat today**, so it must win on Vietnamese language quality, local integrations (Zalo, Facebook, Shopee, TikTok Shop), VND billing and done-for-you service. Second, the owner must confirm the script's licence allows commercial resale as a SaaS. The asset worth building on is not the code. It is the owner's network, credibility and ability to sell to Vietnamese executives.

The name is also a liability. "Luvix" belongs to at least six unrelated businesses: an AI talking-avatar video tool (luvix.in), a Dutch innovation consultancy that owns the LinkedIn "Luvix" page, a VPN and proxy service, and a Shopify theme ([luvix.in](https://www.luvix.in/); [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/luvix); [luvix.org](https://luvix.org/)). One letter away, **Lovix.ai is an AI girlfriend/companion service** run from Hong Kong with an affiliate programme ([lovix.ai](https://lovix.ai/); [partnerkin](https://partnerkin.com/c/33/lovixai)). A bank CDO who mistypes the name during vendor due diligence could land on that site, and that is a real reputational hazard in a trust-driven enterprise sale.

## Vision: the Vietnamese-first AI team for selling and serving customers

**Proposed vision statement:** *"Luvix gives every Vietnamese business, from a TikTok Shop seller to a national bank, an AI team that writes, sells and serves customers in fluent Vietnamese, on Zalo and Facebook, billed in VND, and compliant with Vietnamese law."*

The vision targets the work Vietnamese firms already pay AI to do. Among SMEs using AI, **66% use it for customer communication and 63% to reach new customers** ([VnEconomy](https://vneconomy.vn/techconnect/thieu-tien-thieu-nguoi-thieu-chien-luoc-bo-ba-rao-can-khien-sme-viet-chua-tan-dung-het-ai.htm)). That points to exactly the content-plus-chatbot product a content writer can grow into. The base of potential buyers is large. Vietnam had **859,048 active enterprises at end-2025** and 6.27 million operating economic units ([VietnamFinance](https://vietnamfinance.vn/tong-dieu-tra-kinh-te-2026-hon-859000-doanh-nghiep-hoat-dong-doanh-thu-453-trieu-ty-dong-d147522.html); [VnEconomy](https://vneconomy.vn/ca-nuoc-co-63-trieu-co-so-kinh-te-dang-hoat-dong-va-co-ket-qua-san-xuat-kinh-doanh.htm)). The digital economy reached about **US$39B GMV in 2025**, of which about US$25B was e-commerce ([VnEconomy](https://vneconomy.vn/google-nen-kinh-te-so-viet-nam-du-kien-dat-39-ty-usd-vao-nam-2025.htm)). Headline AI adoption looks high: Deloitte reports 93% of SMEs use AI ([Advertising Vietnam](https://advertisingvietnam.com/article/viet-nam-dan-dau-dong-nam-a-ve-ung-dung-ai-cac-doanh-nghiep-sme-but-toc-trong-cuoc-dua-so-hoa-p26641)). But that figure almost certainly counts free ChatGPT use. The binding constraints are **money, people and strategy** ([VnEconomy](https://vneconomy.vn/techconnect/thieu-tien-thieu-nguoi-thieu-chien-luoc-bo-ba-rao-can-khien-sme-viet-chua-tan-dung-het-ai.htm)). Vietnamese SMEs will pay for outcomes (more replies answered, more listings live, more leads followed up), not for a tool that writes words.

At the top of the market, the gap is getting from pilot to production. AWS estimates **61% of Vietnamese AI applications are still at exploration or pilot stage and only about 8% are integrated into business processes** ([VCCI](https://vcci.com.vn/news/doanh-nghiep-viet-tang-toc-ung-dung-ai)). A CIO Summit 2026 survey found **97% of enterprises still experimenting** ([VietTimes](https://viettimes.vn/nhieu-doanh-nghiep-viet-dau-tu-vao-ai-nhung-khong-dat-duoc-ky-vong-post180104.html)). Over 60% of CxOs plan to spend more on AI ([VCCI](https://vcci.com.vn/news/doanh-nghiep-viet-tang-toc-ung-dung-ai)), and finance and banking lead adoption at 41% versus a 26% average. A former CEO/CFO/CDO is unusually well placed to sell this promise: *"we take one AI use case from pilot to production in 90 days, with a number your CFO signs off on."* That is the enterprise half of the vision.

Over five-plus years, the arc runs in four stages. First, a SME product earns volume, feedback and recurring revenue. Second, Luvix moves into mid-market chains (retail, F&B, real estate, education). Third, it sells enterprise deployments in banking, insurance, telecom and conglomerate ecosystems. Fourth, it expands into Southeast Asia. Vietnamese precedents show each layer can be large. **Base.vn** grew from 500+ customers with logos such as VIB, VPBank and McDonald's in 2018 to **9,600+ customers** before FPT bought a majority stake ([Bizhub](https://bizhub.vietnamnews.vn/basevn-raises-13-million-post301612.html); [Thị trường Tài chính Tiền tệ](https://thitruongtaichinhtiente.vn/saas-day-2024-bien-cong-nghe-thanh-chia-khoa-tang-truong-cho-hon-800-000-doanh-nghiep-tai-viet-nam-64286.html); [Báo Pháp luật](https://doanhnhan.baophapluat.vn/deal-street-asia-eddie-thai-rut-von-fpt-chinh-thuc-mua-lai-phan-lon-co-phan-cua-start-up-basevn-40081.html)). **MISA** serves 214,000+ businesses ([Thương hiệu & Công luận](https://thuonghieucongluan.com.vn/misa-doanh-nghiep-dan-dau-ve-giai-phap-tai-chinh-ke-toan-quan-tri-doanh-nghiep-va-phat-trien-ai-a284789.html)). FPT recognised **913 billion VND of AI and cloud revenue in 2025, up 48.4%** ([FPT](https://fpt.com/en/news/fpt-annual-report-2025-ai-becomes-a-core-capability-in-growth-strategy)). The "make a lot of money" outcome follows these paths: a recurring SME base that an FPT-, MISA- or VCCorp-type group wants to acquire, or a specialist application layer that big infrastructure players resell.

## Segments, packaging and VND pricing built for how Vietnamese firms buy

### Five segments, ranked by how quickly they pay

Enterprise accounts reached through the owner's network come first, because one deal can be worth more than a hundred SMEs. The best targets are banks, insurers and securities firms (the most mature AI buyers), retail and consumer chains needing marketing content and customer service at scale, and conglomerate ecosystems. A narrow, measurable use case wins the door-opener: customer-service or sales chatbots on Zalo OA and the website, marketing content at volume across hundreds of SKUs or branches, or an internal knowledge assistant. **TikTok Shop and Shopee sellers** come next. TikTok Shop had **266,000+ sellers with revenue in H1 2025, up 96%**, and Shopee 209,000 ([Báo Pháp Luật](https://doanhnhan.baophapluat.vn/thi-truong-tmdt-6-thang-dau-nam-2025-tiktok-shop-vuot-shopee-ve-so-luong-nha-ban-84838.html)). More than 600,000 shops exited in 2025 ([Báo Pháp Luật](https://doanhnhan.baophapluat.vn/hon-600-000-shop-chia-tay-thi-truong-thuong-mai-dien-tu-trong-nam-2025-da-tang-cua-shopee-cham-lai.html)), so the survivors are more professional and better able to pay. Third are **digital agencies and KOC/affiliate networks**, which produce content at scale and can resell. Fourth are **lead-driven local services**: spas and clinics, real estate brokers and education centres that live on Facebook and Zalo and lose leads to slow replies. Fifth is F&B for bookings, where Bizfly already sells a restaurant chatbot at 1.8M VND per six months ([Bizfly](https://bizfly.vn/giai-phap/bizfly-nha-hang.html)).

### Price above the chatbot pack, below the consultants

Local tools set the SME price anchor low. Fchat lists 299,000–999,000 VND/month ([fchat.vn](https://fchat.vn/price)). Smax runs about 499k–999k ([CloudGO](https://cloudgo.vn/phan-mem-chatbot)), BizChatAI 300k–500k ([Bizfly](https://bizfly.vn/techblog/bang-gia-chatbot-gia-re-hop-ly-pho-bien-nhat-hien-nay.html)) and Haravan about 300k–800k ([MISA eShop](https://www.misaeshop.vn/?p=24283)). ChatGPT Plus costs about **527k–550k VND/month** and requires an international card ([gptprompts.ai](https://gptprompts.ai/vi/gia-chatgpt)), which makes roughly 500k a psychological ceiling for a single-user AI tool. Prepaid 6- and 12-month plans are normal ([DooPage](https://doopage.com/vi/danh-gia-co-ban-ve-phan-mem-pancake/)). Luvix should therefore not compete at 299k on a commodity writer. It should price on the outcome and the service wrapped around it.

| Package | Who | What it includes | Proposed VND price |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Luvix Starter** | Solo sellers, micro-shops | Vietnamese templates for Facebook, TikTok, Shopee/Lazada listings; AI chat; credit cap | 299k/month, or 2.99M/year (2 months free) |
| **Luvix Business** | SMEs, small agencies | 3–5 seats, brand voice, shared credit pool, bulk product-listing generator, content calendar | 1.5M/month, or 15M/year |
| **Luvix Sales Bot (done-for-you)** | Spas, real estate, education, F&B, e-commerce | AI chatbot trained on the customer's data, deployed on Zalo OA, Messenger and website, human handoff, monthly tuning; includes Business | 15–25M setup + 4M/month (first year about 63–73M) |
| **Agency / reseller** | Agencies, Sapo/Haravan implementers | White-label workspace, client sub-accounts, 20–30% recurring commission | 5–8M/month, or revenue share |
| **Enterprise pilot → deployment** | Banks, insurers, chains, groups | 6–8 week fixed-scope paid POC with written success metrics and a pre-agreed rollout price; Vietnamese hosting option; DPA; SLA | POC 100–200M; production 300–800M+/year |

The done-for-you tier is the commercial heart of the SME business. Vietnamese SaaS leaders grew through sales-assisted, relationship-heavy motions, and SMB monthly churn of **3–7%** on monthly plans ([Vena Solutions](https://www.venasolutions.com/blog/saas-churn-rate)) punishes pure self-serve. Monthly contracts churn **3–5x** faster than annual ones ([Stealth Agents](https://stealthagents.com/research/saas-churn-rate-statistics-2026)). Every sales script should therefore push for annual prepaid billing with a setup fee. That pulls cash forward and removes most churn risk within the 1-billion window.

## The revenue math: thirty-odd customers and two pilots reach 1 billion VND

A pure self-serve route is arithmetically unattractive. At 500k VND/month, 1B VND needs **2,000 paid customer-months**, roughly 83 customers kept active for two full years while replacing 5% of them each month. At 5M VND/month it needs only 200 customer-months. The plan below therefore weights revenue toward high-value contracts and treats Starter as a lead source, not a revenue engine. The figures are this report's own planning arithmetic built on the pricing above, not market data.

| Revenue line (cumulative, months 1–18) | Units | Value per unit | Booked revenue |
|---|---|---|---|
| Enterprise paid POCs | 2 | 150M | 300M |
| Enterprise POC converted to annual deployment | 1 | 400M | 400M |
| Sales Bot (done-for-you), first-year contracts | 6 | 63M | 378M |
| Business annual plans | 12 | 15M | 180M |
| Starter annual plans | 40 | 3M | 120M |
| **Gross booked** | | | **1,378M** |
| Less partner/reseller commissions (about 20% of SME lines) | | | about −135M |
| **Net toward milestone** | | | **about 1,240M** |

The model carries roughly 25% headroom, so it survives one thing going wrong. Without the enterprise conversion, the remaining lines still reach about 840M net, and two or three more Sales Bot customers close the gap. With **no enterprise revenue at all**, Luvix needs about 15 Sales Bot customers plus 30 Business plans, which is feasible but slower and depends on a repeatable SME sales machine that does not yet exist. The sensitivity is plain: **the owner's network is the swing factor.** Typical Vietnamese AI projects of 1–3B VND ([Mekong ASEAN](https://mekongasean.vn/von-dau-tu-vao-doanh-nghiep-ai-viet-nam-tang-8-lan-47205.html)) mean a single full deployment could deliver the milestone alone. The same survey shows the market is polarised, with **43% of AI-adopting organisations not yet investing and 30% spending under 50M VND**. Enterprise pipeline must therefore be qualified hard for budget.

Getting well beyond 1B VND follows the same structure at larger scale. To illustrate: 1,500 SMEs averaging 15M VND a year is about 22.5B VND, and 20 enterprise accounts averaging 1.5B is another 30B, giving a business in the **50B VND+ annual recurring range**. That is the size at which Vietnamese B2B software companies attract strategic buyers or institutional investors, as Base.vn, KiotViet (US$45M from KKR) and Cinnamon AI did ([Tuoi Tre News](https://tuoitrenews.vn/news/business/20210904/vietnams-startup-kiotviet-raises-45-mn-from-global-investment-firm/62929.html); [Startup Intros](https://startupintros.com/orgs/cinnamon)). Reaching it needs the stage-by-stage expansion described in the vision section, plus a proprietary layer such as Vietnamese-tuned workflows, integrations and customer data, which a reseller script cannot provide.

## An 18-month roadmap that front-loads the owner's network

### Phase 0, weeks 0–6: make Luvix sellable in Vietnam

Nothing can be sold until the basics exist. Confirm what the product is and who owns the code and licence. Decide on the name (keep "Luvix" with defensive domains and SEO, or rebrand before spending on marketing). Register or confirm a Vietnamese JSC or LLC with an IT/software business line. Ship a Vietnamese UI and Vietnamese-quality output. Publish VND pricing. Set up VietQR collection through a gateway such as payOS, which generates a QR per order and confirms payment by webhook ([Odoo Apps](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/17.0/payment_payos)). Contract a licensed e-invoice provider, and prepare a Vietnamese service contract, NDA and data processing agreement. **Milestone:** a working demo in Vietnamese, a price list, the ability to invoice, and a list of 30 named executives to call.

### Phase 1, months 1–4: founder-led design partners

The owner personally works the network. The aim is 15–20 C-level conversations, from which come **two paid POCs** with success metrics agreed in writing and a pre-agreed production price. In parallel, recruit **5–10 SME design partners** in a single vertical (spas and clinics, or TikTok Shop sellers) on discounted annual Sales Bot deals in exchange for case studies. This is the Base.vn logo-first playbook ([Bizhub](https://bizhub.vietnamnews.vn/basevn-raises-13-million-post301612.html)). Hire or contract one implementation engineer and one Vietnamese-speaking customer-success lead. **Milestone:** 250–350M VND booked, two written case studies, and a measured before/after metric such as reply time or leads converted.

### Phase 2, months 4–9: productise and add channels

Turn what the design partners needed into standard Sales Bot features: Zalo OA and Messenger deployment, human handoff and a Vietnamese knowledge base. Competitors such as Sapo already offer chatbots across Facebook, Shopee, Zalo and Instagram ([Sapo](https://www.sapo.vn/cap-nhat-tinh-nang/tich-hop-instagram-zalo-zalo-oa-vao-chatbot-ai)), so this is table stakes. Sign **3–5 agency and reseller partners** on 20–30% recurring commission. Apply to the Sapo and Haravan app ecosystems: Sapo says partners can reach **230,000+ merchants** ([Sapo](https://www.sapo.vn/nha-phat-trien-ung-dung.html)), and Haravan runs an app platform ([Haravan docs](https://docs.haravan.com/docs/tutorials/authentication/apps-overview/)). Run monthly Vietnamese workshops and live demos to answer SMEs' doubts about cost and ROI ([VOV](https://kynguyenvuonminh.vov.vn/en/economy/smes-cautious-about-ai-adoption-amid-efficiency-concerns-post1207935.vov)). **Milestone:** 600M VND cumulative, at least one enterprise POC converted, and a first partner-sourced deal.

### Phase 3, months 9–18: cross 1 billion VND and build the machine

Add a second enterprise wave from the case studies, expand the first enterprise account into other departments and subsidiaries, and hire one or two salespeople for SME and partner accounts. Start a renewal and upsell motion modelled on Base.vn's account-executive structure ([TopCV](https://www.topcv.vn/viec-lam/customer-development-account-executive-b2b-saas-renewal-upsell-cross-sell/2259466.html)). Where a bank or state-owned enterprise needs tender eligibility or local hosting, partner with FPT, CMC, Viettel or VNPT, or with a systems integrator, and accept shared margin. **Milestone:** 1B VND cumulative by month 12–15 (stretch) or month 18 (base case), more than 70% of SME revenue on annual contracts, and logo churn under 2% a month.

### Phase 4, months 18–24: from milestone to company

Target a **2–3B VND annual run-rate**. Add a second vertical. Formalise one infrastructure partnership on Vietnamese cloud. Decide whether to raise seed capital on the strength of the logos and recurring revenue. Investment into Vietnamese AI companies rose from about US$10M in 2023 to about US$80M in 2024 ([Mekong ASEAN](https://mekongasean.vn/von-dau-tu-vao-doanh-nghiep-ai-viet-nam-tang-8-lan-47205.html)), so capital exists for credible teams.

### Channels in order of expected yield

Channel priorities follow how Vietnamese buyers behave. For the first year, the top channel is **the owner's warm introductions**. Next comes **Zalo messages followed by a Vietnamese phone call**, which one vendor reports gets the highest response rates ([Origami](https://origami.chat/blog/find-local-businesses-without-websites-vietnam-b2b-sales)). Then come Facebook and Zalo groups for each vertical, together with TikTok demo videos. Agency, reseller and app-marketplace partners follow. Google-only plans miss buyers who vet brands inside local apps ([Elite Asia](https://www.eliteasia.co/?p=38923)). LinkedIn matters mainly for enterprise and foreign-invested firms. No source gave Vietnam-specific conversion or acquisition-cost data by channel, so Luvix should measure its own channels from the first month.

## Legal must-dos, risks, and what changes if the product is different

### Six compliance items to complete before the first enterprise contract

Selling AI to Vietnamese businesses in 2026 means working under four new regimes plus standard contract hygiene.

| Must-do | Rule | What it requires of Luvix |
|---|---|---|
| Personal data compliance | **PDP Law 91/2025/QH15**, in force 1 Jan 2026; GDPR-style; cross-border transfer breaches fined up to **5% of prior-year revenue** ([Baker McKenzie](https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/alerts/2025/10/vietnam-decoding-the-pdp-law-gdpr-inspired-rules-with-local-twist); [Tilleke & Gibbins](https://www.tilleke.com/insights/vietnams-new-personal-data-protection-law-a-closer-look/2/)) | Vietnamese privacy notice, consent handling, a cross-border transfer dossier for every foreign LLM API call that carries end-customer personal data |
| Implementing decree | **Decree 356/2025** replaces Decree 13/2023 with more concrete duties ([Rajah & Tann](https://www.rajahtannasia.com/viewpoints/new-decree-implementing-the-law-on-personal-data-protection-passed/)) | Counsel review of processor obligations and any licence requirement |
| AI transparency | **AI Law 134/2025/QH15**, in force 1 Mar 2026; three risk tiers; disclose AI interaction; label synthetic content imitating real people ([Duane Morris](https://blogs.duanemorris.com/vietnam/2026/03/03/vietnam-the-first-law-on-artificial-intelligence-what-you-must-know/)) | "You are chatting with an AI assistant" notice in every bot; written risk self-classification per feature |
| E-invoicing | **Decree 70/2025**, in force 1 Jun 2025; signed XML invoices through the tax system ([KPMG](https://kpmg.com/us/en/taxnewsflash/news/2025/04/tnf-vietnam-amendments-to-regulations-on-electronic-invoices.html)) | Licensed e-invoice provider from the first sale |
| VAT | **VAT Law 48/2024** makes software products and services non-VAT from 1 Jul 2025 ([PwC](https://www.pwc.com/vn/en/publications/2024/241216-new-vat-law.pdf)), but "software leasing" has been treated as taxable ([Apolat Legal](https://apolatlegal.com/notes-on-determining-the-applicable-tax-rate-software-leasing-services/)) | Accountant confirms how a SaaS subscription is classified before contracts are templated |
| Contracts | Luvix is usually the data processor | Vietnamese service contract, NDA and data processing agreement; Vietnamese-cloud hosting option (FPT, Viettel, VNPT) for banks and state-owned firms |

The cross-border flow of customer data to foreign LLMs is probably Luvix's biggest single compliance exposure, and it is the first thing a bank's security review will ask about. A draft-based summary suggests startups may be exempt from some data-protection obligations for five years ([KPMG](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmg/vn/pdf/2025/06/new-pdp-law-en.pdf)), but counsel must confirm this against the final text. The State Bank of Vietnam is also consulting on a draft circular on AI risk management in banking ([VNBA](https://vnba.org.vn/vi/ai-la--chia-khoa--cho-ngan-hang-so-viet-nam-but-pha-19065.htm)).

### Risks that could break the plan

The **largest risk is the product itself.** A script-based writer with euro pricing and no Vietnamese interface cannot be sold to a Vietnamese CDO as-is, and it has no defensible technology. Mitigation: invest the first months in Vietnamese quality and Zalo/Facebook integration, and sell the outcome and service rather than the software. **Name confusion with Lovix.ai**, the AI girlfriend service, plus five other "Luvix" brands, threatens enterprise trust and makes search ownership impossible. Mitigation: decide on a rebrand before spending on marketing, or buy defensive domains and use a descriptive tagline such as "Luvix — AI bán hàng cho doanh nghiệp Việt". **Price compression** from local chatbots at 299k–999k and from free ChatGPT will squeeze any product sold as "an AI writer". **Pilot purgatory** is the enterprise counterpart: with 97% of enterprises still experimenting, unpaid or open-ended POCs waste the founder's scarcest asset, their time. Paid, time-boxed POCs with pre-agreed production pricing are non-negotiable. **Concentration risk** follows from the barbell: one enterprise account could be 40% of revenue. **Incumbents** (FPT.AI with 200+ customers and 10,220 deployed agents, plus Viettel, VNPT and CMC) can bundle AI into much larger relationships ([FPT.AI](https://fpt.ai/?p=20358)), so Luvix should position itself as a specialist partner to them, not a rival. Finally, **LLM cost and dependency** on foreign APIs affects both margins and compliance.

### If Luvix is not the product described here

If the owner's Luvix is a **video or avatar generator** like luvix.in, the plan shifts toward TikTok Shop sellers and agencies. Competitors become HeyGen, CapCut and local voice vendors such as Viettel AI, whose text-to-speech starts at 20,000 VND/month ([viettelai.vn](https://viettelai.vn/en/bang-gia)). The AI Law's labelling duty for synthetic people becomes central. If Luvix is a **custom or enterprise AI build** with its own technology, the enterprise side of the barbell should carry 70–80% of the plan, and SME self-serve can wait. If Luvix is a **brand with no product yet**, the choice is to build on a licensed script or partner with a Vietnamese LLM or chatbot platform, and the name decision becomes urgent. In every case, the pricing ladder, the annual-prepay rule, the paid-POC discipline and the compliance list still apply.

## Conclusion

The research changes how the question should be framed. Luvix's problem is not finding a market. Vietnamese businesses clearly want AI for customer communication, and enterprises have budgets and stalled pilots. The problem is that Luvix, as publicly visible, is a generic, foreign-language, possibly scripted writer with a confusable name. Its most valuable asset is not on the website at all: it is an owner who can get an executive meeting where FPT's account managers cannot. The winning move is to **sell outcomes through that network first and let the product catch up behind the contracts**, rather than polish a self-serve tool and hope SMEs find it.

That also redefines the 1-billion milestone. It is less a revenue target than proof of a repeatable motion: two paid pilots, one production conversion and a few dozen annual SME contracts with case studies. If Luvix reaches it with clean contracts, Vietnamese-hosted data and named logos, it becomes a credible specialist partner or acquisition target for the large groups now spending heavily on AI. If it reaches it only through one-off favours from the owner's network, the company has learned that the network sells but the product does not. The roadmap's real job is to tell those two outcomes apart by month 9.
