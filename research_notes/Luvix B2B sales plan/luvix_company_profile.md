## What does Luvix.ai do? (product, features, AI capabilities, use cases)

### Takeaway
The indexed luvix.ai is a self-serve AI content-writing SaaS. It offers 60+ writing templates (blog/SEO, ads, email, website copy, social media, YouTube, e-commerce), an AI chat, and a set of persona chatbots. It is written mainly in French with a partial English version.

### Cited Findings
- Positioned as an AI content writer for bloggers, marketers and site owners. It promises SEO-optimized, unique content for blogs, ads, emails and websites, and claims a "10x" time saving — [luvix.ai homepage (FR)](https://luvix.ai/); [luvix.ai/home/en](https://luvix.ai/home/en)
- "Generate your required content with over 60+ content creation templates." Template categories in the index include social media, Facebook, YouTube (video scripts, ad scripts, series ideas), TikTok, Twitter/tweets, copywriting, e-commerce and SEO blog articles — [luvix.ai](https://luvix.ai/); [luvix.ai/home/en](https://luvix.ai/home/en)
- A separate line on the pricing section lists "72 AI document templates" (this conflicts with the "60+" figure; both may be marketing copy) — [luvix.ai/home/en](https://luvix.ai/home/en)
- Specialized chatbots "across various industries": relationship advisor, business coach, motivational speaker, life coach, lawyer, doctor. Plans also include "AI chat" — [luvix.ai/home/en](https://luvix.ai/home/en)
- Language: the main site title and pages are in French ("Connexion", "Politiques de confidentialités"). The English page is only partly translated and still shows French text — [luvix.ai/login](https://luvix.ai/login); [luvix.ai/home/en](https://luvix.ai/home/en)
- Free start, "no credit card required" — [luvix.ai](https://luvix.ai/)

### Inferences
- The feature set closely matches the off-the-shelf "AI writer SaaS" scripts sold on marketplaces such as CodeCanyon: "60+ templates", AI chat, persona chatbots, "membership vs prepaid plans", Small/Medium/Large word packs, and a `/page/<slug>` URL pattern. The site may run on a white-label script rather than proprietary tech. This is unconfirmed and the user should verify it.
- I found no proof of a Vietnamese-language interface or a Vietnam-focused product. If the user's Luvix is a Vietnam business, the indexed site may be an older or different version, or a different product.

### Gaps
- I could not see the live site, so the current product, current UI languages and any AI-model disclosures (e.g., which LLMs it uses) remain unverified.
- I found no image, voice, video or API features in the index.

## Pricing and plans

### Takeaway
The indexed pricing is in euros (€). It has a 5-day trial, a €97/year plan, a €199 lifetime plan, and prepaid word packs of €5/€10/€15. I found no team, business or enterprise plan.

### Cited Findings
- Trial: 5 days, 5,000 words/month, AI chat, 4 chatbots — [luvix.ai/home/en](https://luvix.ai/home/en)
- Yearly membership: €97/year, 30,000 words/month — [luvix.ai/home/en](https://luvix.ai/home/en)
- Lifetime: €199 one-time, 130,000 words/month — [luvix.ai/home/en](https://luvix.ai/home/en)
- Prepaid word packs: Small €5 for 60,000 words (labeled "Recommended"), Medium €10 for 80,000 words, Large €15 for 100,000 words — [luvix.ai/home/en](https://luvix.ai/home/en)
- The page has "membership plans" and "prepaid plans" tabs. A partly translated FAQ tells visitors to contact the company for exact pricing — [luvix.ai/home/en](https://luvix.ai/home/en)

### Inferences
- The model is words-based credits, aimed at individuals and freelancers. A lifetime deal and very cheap packs suggest early-stage, AppSumo-style pricing. The pack prices look odd: the €5 pack gives more words than the yearly plan's monthly allowance. That suggests placeholder or unrevised numbers.
- No VND pricing and no local payment methods (MoMo, VNPay, bank transfer) appear.

### Gaps
- Current prices, and whether the plans still exist, are unverified. I found no seat-based, team, API or enterprise tier.

## Is there a B2B offering? Which features could be packaged for businesses?

### Takeaway
I found no B2B, team or enterprise offering. The existing features could be packaged for businesses: templates for ads, email, e-commerce and SEO, plus persona chatbots.

### Cited Findings
- Plans are individual only (trial / yearly / lifetime / word packs). None mention seats, teams, invoices or an API — [luvix.ai/home/en](https://luvix.ai/home/en)
- Feature inventory usable for B2B: SEO blog articles, ad copy, email copy, website copy, e-commerce product content, Facebook/TikTok/YouTube scripts, and industry chatbots (business coach, lawyer, doctor) — [luvix.ai](https://luvix.ai/); [luvix.ai/home/en](https://luvix.ai/home/en)
- A Vietnamese benchmark: LovinBot AI sells a white-label, no-code AI platform to businesses, experts and agencies in Vietnam — [lovinbot.ai](https://lovinbot.ai/); [VnExpress](https://vnexpress.net/lovinbot-ai-nen-tang-so-hoa-ai-cho-doanh-nghiep-4914035.html). Luviet.com markets AI SEO article writing to Vietnamese businesses — [luviet.com](https://www.luviet.com/2025/12/luvietcom-ong-hanh-cung-doanh-nghiep.html)

### Inferences (candidate B2B packages, not existing offers)
- **Agency/SME content plan:** shared word pool, multiple seats, brand voice, and Vietnamese-language templates for Facebook, TikTok, Shopee and Lazada product listings.
- **Custom/white-label chatbot:** turn the persona-chatbot feature into a company-trained customer-support or sales bot, deployable on Zalo OA, Facebook Messenger or a website.
- **E-commerce bulk listing generator** for sellers on Shopee, Lazada and TikTok Shop.
- These require VND invoicing (VAT e-invoice), local payment methods and a Vietnamese UI, none of which appear on the indexed site.

### Gaps
- I don't know what the real product can do now, e.g. Vietnamese output quality, custom knowledge bases or integrations.
