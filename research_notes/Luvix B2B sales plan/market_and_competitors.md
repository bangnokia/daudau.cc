## 2. Which Vietnamese business segments most need this kind of tool, and why?

### Takeaway
Vietnamese SMEs use AI mainly for customer-facing work: talking to customers and finding new ones. The most valuable segment is online sellers on social commerce (TikTok Shop and Shopee). They need high volumes of short video content and fast replies to chat messages, and their numbers grew sharply in 2025.

### Cited Findings
- 66% of Vietnamese SMEs that use AI use it for customer communication, and 63% use it to reach new customers. — [VnEconomy, Jul 2026](https://vneconomy.vn/techconnect/thieu-tien-thieu-nguoi-thieu-chien-luoc-bo-ba-rao-can-khien-sme-viet-chua-tan-dung-het-ai.htm)
- TikTok Shop had more than 266,000 sellers with revenue in H1 2025, up 96% year on year. Shopee had 209,000 domestic sellers. — [Báo Pháp Luật / Doanh nhân](https://doanhnhan.baophapluat.vn/thi-truong-tmdt-6-thang-dau-nam-2025-tiktok-shop-vuot-shopee-ve-so-luong-nha-ban-84838.html)
- Market consolidation: total active shops fell 7.43% to about 601,800 in 2025, and more than 600,000 shops left e-commerce during the year. — [Báo Pháp Luật / Doanh nhân](https://doanhnhan.baophapluat.vn/hon-600-000-shop-chia-tay-thi-truong-thuong-mai-dien-tu-trong-nam-2025-da-tang-cua-shopee-cham-lai.html); [Đại biểu Nhân dân](https://daibieunhandan.vn/toan-canh-thuong-mai-dien-tu-2025-cuoc-dua-song-ma-va-su-thanh-loc-khac-nghiet-10404029.html)
- Metric data: Shopee held about 56% of 2025 e-commerce sales (64% in 2024). TikTok Shop rose from 29% to 41.31%. — [Đại biểu Nhân dân](https://daibieunhandan.vn/thuong-mai-dien-tu-viet-nam-2025-lazada-va-tiki-tut-doc-chia-nhau-3-thi-phan-10405406.html); [MISA eShop report](https://www.misaeshop.vn/bao-cao-thuong-mai-dien-tu-2025/)
- Vertical demand: Bizfly sells a chatbot product built for restaurant table booking and ordering, at 1,800,000 VND for 6 months. This points to demand from F&B. — [Bizfly](https://bizfly.vn/giai-phap/bizfly-nha-hang.html)
- Sapo extended its AI chatbot to Zalo, Zalo OA and Instagram, in addition to Facebook and Shopee, with handoff to a human agent. Retail and e-commerce sellers expect support across many channels. — [Sapo, Apr 2025](https://www.sapo.vn/cap-nhat-tinh-nang/tich-hop-instagram-zalo-zalo-oa-vao-chatbot-ai)

### Inferences
- Priority segments, in order:
  1. TikTok Shop and Shopee sellers and livestream sellers: video volume, product descriptions, chat replies.
  2. Agencies and KOC/affiliate networks that produce content at scale.
  3. Spas, beauty clinics, real estate brokers and education centers, which generate leads on Facebook and Zalo and need fast replies plus ad creatives.
  4. F&B for bookings.
- Consolidation in e-commerce (fewer sellers, higher GMV per seller) suggests the remaining sellers are more professional and more able to pay.

### Gaps
- I found no Vietnam data on AI tool adoption by industry vertical (spa, real estate, education).

## 3. Market size indicators

### Takeaway
Vietnam had about 859,000 active enterprises at end-2025 (about 98% are SMEs) and about 6.27 million operating economic units, most of them household businesses. Reported AI adoption among SMEs is high. The digital economy was about USD 39B GMV in 2025, of which about USD 25B was e-commerce.

## 4. Competitors in Vietnam (direct and indirect)

### Takeaway
Vietnamese chatbot and inbox tools are cheap, roughly 199k–999k VND/month, and well established: Fchat, Smax, Pancake, Bizfly, Sapo, Subiz. Vietnamese AI voice vendors (Viettel AI, Vbee, FPT.AI) sell usage-based plans. International AI tools such as ChatGPT Plus cost about 527k–550k VND/month, must be paid by international card, and set the benchmark for what an SME considers a "premium" price.

### Cited Findings
**A. AI chatbot and sales automation (Vietnamese)**

- **FPT.AI**: no official price list found. A secondary blog estimates about 200–1,000 VND per API call. — [apifeny blog](https://apifeny-ai.vercel.app/blog/best-ai-tools-vietnam-2026)

**C. International tools (indirect)**
- **ChatGPT Plus**: USD 20/month, about 527,000 VND at about 26,350 VND/USD (May 2026), or 530k–550k VND including card fees. Requires an international card; MoMo, ZaloPay and QR were not accepted as of May 2026. — [gptprompts.ai](https://gptprompts.ai/vi/gia-chatgpt); [Finhay](https://www.finhay.com.vn/chatgpt-plus-gia-bao-nhieu)

### Inferences
- **Strengths of local players:** Vietnamese language, Zalo integration (international tools often lack it), VND billing by bank transfer or e-wallet, and bundling with POS and order management.
- **Weaknesses of local players:** mostly rule-based or older bot technology now adding LLM features, and limited generative video.
- **Weaknesses of international tools:** card-only payment, USD pricing, weak Vietnamese voice and accent, and no Zalo.
- **Opening for Luvix:** a Vietnamese-first generative AI tool covering content or video plus chat, paid in VND by QR or e-wallet.

### Gaps
- HeyGen, Synthesia, CapCut Pro and Canva Pro prices in Vietnam were not verified this session; searches returned nothing. From background knowledge, HeyGen and Synthesia entry plans have been around USD 24–29/month, but this is unverified and should be checked before use.
- I found no market-share data for Vietnamese chatbot vendors, and no Vietnamese vendors of AI video avatars or UGC generation.

## 6. Large enterprises and corporate groups as buyers (banks, conglomerates, telecom, retail chains, manufacturing) — long-term vision

### Takeaway
Large Vietnamese enterprises are raising AI budgets, but most deployments are still at pilot stage. Finance and banking lead adoption: 41% vs a 26% average. The enterprise market is dominated by domestic tech groups (FPT, Viettel, VNPT, CMC, VinBigData) that sell infrastructure and custom solutions, with Microsoft (via FPT) as the main international channel. A Luvix enterprise play would need a stack that is compliant, can run on-prem or on sovereign cloud, and handles Vietnamese well. Partnering with these groups is a realistic route alongside competing with them.

### Inferences
- **Enterprise opportunity:** pilots are common but production deployments are rare (about 8% integrated). The gap is "pilot to production". A vendor that offers fast time-to-value, Vietnamese-language quality and governance aligned with the draft SBV AI Circular could win. Banking, insurance and securities are the most mature buyers. Retail chains and consumer conglomerates (marketing content, customer service at scale) and telecom (contact centers) come next.
- **Likely enterprise buying pattern:** pilot or POC → departmental license → group-wide framework agreement. Procurement goes through tenders and requires data residency, on-prem or Vietnamese-cloud deployment (Viettel IDC, FPT Cloud, VNPT), and VAT invoicing. A founder with CEO/CFO/CDO credentials and access to C-suite networks is a real advantage in this sales motion.
- **Partner vs compete:** FPT, Viettel, CMC and VNPT have infrastructure, compute and relationships, but their applications are horizontal. A specialised application vendor can work as a partner or reseller on their clouds or marketplaces, or as a subcontractor in system-integrator deals.
- **Long-term arc:** start with SME self-serve for volume and product feedback. Move to mid-market (retail chains, F&B chains, real estate developers, education groups). Then target enterprise (banks, conglomerate ecosystems such as Vingroup, Masan, Sun Group, telecoms). Then expand regionally into SEA.
