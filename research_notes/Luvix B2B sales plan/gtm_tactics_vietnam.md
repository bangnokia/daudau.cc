# Go-to-Market and Sales Tactics for an Early-Stage AI SaaS Selling to Vietnamese Businesses (Luvix.ai, target 1 billion VND cumulative B2B revenue)

Research note, compiled 2026-10-14. Method: about 16 web searches and fetch attempts. Important limitation: full-page fetches failed with DNS errors for several domains (tilleke.com, bakermckenzie.com, luatvietnam.vn, apolatlegal.com, about.kiotviet.vn), so many findings below rest on search-result snippets and summaries rather than full-text reads. Vietnam-specific quantitative SaaS benchmarks (conversion, CAC, sales cycle) were not found. Global benchmarks are used as proxies and labeled that way.

## 1. Which acquisition channels work best for B2B SaaS to Vietnamese SMEs?

### Takeaway
The evidence points to messaging-led selling: Zalo (OA plus personal Zalo for 1:1 follow-up) and Facebook, combined with phone calls in Vietnamese and distribution through ecosystems and partners (Sapo, Haravan, KiotViet-style platforms). Google-only or LinkedIn-only plans miss most SME buyers. No source gave hard Vietnam-specific channel conversion data, so all channel rankings are directional.

### Cited Findings
- Zalo is Vietnam's dominant messaging "super app" (messaging, payments, e-commerce). One cited figure says 75.8% of people aged 16-64 used Zalo in 2020, against 76.5% for Facebook Messenger. The figure is dated. — [Chative blog](https://chative.io/blog/how-to-effectively-reach-customers-and-boost-sales-on-zalo-for-businesses/)
- A vendor blog forecasts that messaging will become the main sales channel for Vietnamese SMEs in 2026, and says customers prefer texting to calling. This is vendor opinion. — [Zenify](https://zenify.vn/blog/messaging-commerce-vietnam)
- The phone call is still the dominant channel for local B2B sales, with a shift toward Zalo voice and video calls. The highest response rates reportedly come from a brief Zalo message intro followed by a phone call in Vietnamese. This is one vendor's anecdote. — [Origami](https://origami.chat/blog/find-local-businesses-without-websites-vietnam-b2b-sales)
- A Google-only plan misses part of the buyer journey: Vietnamese professionals check brands inside local apps and message peers on Zalo. — [Elite Asia](https://www.eliteasia.co/?p=38923)
- B2B MSME platforms such as Kilo let shop owners manage sales across their own app, Facebook and Zalo, which shows where SME owners actually operate. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2021/11/14/vietnamese-b2b-e-commerce-platform-kilo-bags-5m-pre-series-a)
- Vietnamese CRM leaders compete on Zalo OA and Fanpage integration plus email, SMS and Zalo ZNS nurturing. Getfly's positioning is "CRM + Marketing Automation" with Fanpage and Zalo OA integration. — [Brands Vietnam](https://www.brandsvietnam.com/congdong/topic/top-9-phan-mem-crm-tai-viet-nam-ban-nen-thu-2026)
- Subiz (Hanoi live chat, founded 2013) supports live chat, email, Messenger, Facebook Page comments and Zalo OA. Fchat (SaleMall, part of Inet Group) integrates Messenger, Zalo, website and Lazada. Multichannel Zalo and Facebook support is table stakes for Vietnamese SME tools. — [CMSWire](https://www.cmswire.com/d/subiz-o001520); [VnExpress](https://vnexpress.net/fchat-chatbot-giai-phap-cham-soc-khach-hang-da-kenh-4702037.html)
- Ecosystem and marketplace channels:
  - Sapo runs a partner program for app developers and integrators. It says partner solutions can reach "more than 230,000 Sapo merchants nationwide." — [Sapo partner page](https://www.sapo.vn/nha-phat-trien-ung-dung.html)
  - Sapo's app store already lists AI and Zalo Mini App vendors such as Aimini, a precedent for an AI startup distributing through Sapo. The source is promotional. — [Sapo blog](https://www.sapo.vn/blog/he-thong-tao-mini-app-aimini-sapo)
  - Haravan has a public app platform where merchants install apps in the Haravan admin. — [Haravan docs](https://docs.haravan.com/docs/tutorials/authentication/apps-overview/)
  - Haravan reportedly serves more than 50,000 SMEs and more than 300 large brands. The figure may be dated. — [FPT Shop](https://fptshop.com.vn/tin-tuc/danh-gia/haravan-la-gi-187484)
- SMEs make up over 98% of Vietnam's roughly 800,000 enterprises. — [BizASEAN](https://app.bizasean.com/?p=23619)

### Inferences
- For Luvix, a plausible channel order for months 0-12 is:
  1. Founder-led outbound through Zalo plus phone, using warm networks and Facebook or Zalo groups for the target vertical.
  2. Content and live demos in Facebook groups and on TikTok, showing concrete AI use cases in Vietnamese.
  3. Agency, reseller and affiliate partners (digital marketing agencies, accounting firms, Sapo, Haravan or KiotViet implementation partners) on 20-30% recurring commission.
  4. A listing in the Sapo or Haravan app store once the product integrates.
  SEO, Google Ads and LinkedIn are secondary for SMEs. LinkedIn is useful mainly for mid-market or enterprise buyers and FDI firms.
- Free workshops and webinars in Vietnamese (in person in HCMC or Hanoi, or on Zoom) fit a market where officials report SMEs are hesitant because of cost and unclear ROI (see Section 3). Live demonstration of ROI addresses the main objection.
- Integrating with Zalo OA/ZNS and Facebook Messenger is likely a product requirement, not just a channel. Competitors (Getfly, Subiz, Fchat) all offer it.

### Gaps
- No Vietnam-specific data on CPL, CAC or conversion by channel (Facebook ads vs Zalo ads vs TikTok vs Google).
- Revenue-share terms for the Sapo, Haravan, KiotViet and MISA app marketplaces were not found. Contact their partner teams directly.
- No reliable data on TikTok as a B2B SaaS channel in Vietnam.

## 2. Sales motions: self-serve vs sales-assisted vs done-for-you; conversion, sales cycle, CAC benchmarks

### Takeaway
Vietnamese SaaS leaders grew through sales-assisted and relationship-heavy motions (telesales, demos, onboarding, partner networks) rather than pure self-serve. For an early AI product, sales-assisted plus done-for-you setup is the most realistic route to 1B VND. Global SMB churn benchmarks are high (about 3-7% monthly), and annual contracts cut churn sharply.

### Cited Findings
- SMB SaaS monthly churn is typically 3-7% (30-58% annually), driven by price sensitivity and low loyalty. The figure draws on Recurly and Paddle data. — [Vena Solutions](https://www.venasolutions.com/blog/saas-churn-rate)
- A narrower estimate puts SMB monthly churn at 3-5% (31-46% annually). — [Fungies](https://fungies.io/saas-market-statistics-2026-6/)
- ChartMogul is cited for 7-8% monthly logo churn for very small SMBs (1-50 employees). This is secondary and unverified. — [Stealth Agents](https://stealthagents.com/research/saas-churn-rate-statistics-2026)
- Monthly contracts churn at 3-5x the rate of annual contracts (Recurly data, secondary citation). — [Stealth Agents](https://stealthagents.com/research/saas-churn-rate-statistics-2026)
- A rule of thumb: keeping monthly churn under 2% (about 20% annually) is survivable for SMB products. — [UserJot](https://userjot.com/blog/saas-churn-rate-benchmarks)
- Many growing businesses rush into CRM implementation before their sales processes are mature. This argues for implementation and consulting help rather than pure self-serve. — [BizASEAN](https://app.bizasean.com/?p=23619)

### Inferences
- Recommended motion for Luvix:
  - Sales-assisted pilot: a 7-14 day guided trial set up on the customer's own data.
  - Close with an annual or semi-annual prepaid contract.
  - Add a paid setup or "done-for-you" package (data preparation, prompts, workflow integration, staff training).
- Pure self-serve at low VND price points needs thousands of customer-months to reach 1B VND (see Section 5), which is unrealistic for an early team.
- The SME sales cycle in Vietnam is likely short (days to a few weeks) for sub-5M VND/month deals once a demo happens. This is an inference only; no source found.

### Gaps
- No Vietnam-specific trial-to-paid conversion rates, sales-cycle lengths or CAC benchmarks were found. Global trial-conversion data (OpenView, ChartMogul) could not be retrieved in this session.

## 3. Pricing and packaging; payments; VAT and e-invoices

### Takeaway
Vietnamese SME SaaS is priced in VND, typically in the hundreds of thousands to low millions of VND per month, often billed in prepaid 6-12 month blocks. Buyers are price- and ROI-sensitive. Bank transfer through VietQR is the default payment rail, and payment gateways such as payOS add automated QR and webhook confirmation. Every B2B sale needs a compliant e-invoice under Decree 123/2020 as amended by Decree 70/2025. Software products and services are non-VAT items under the new VAT Law from 1 July 2025, with a caveat for "software leasing."

### Cited Findings
- Getfly CRM's Startup plan was about 521,000 VND/month for 3 users. The figure may be dated. — [Viblo](https://viblo.asia/p/9-crm-cho-doanh-nghiep-nho-pho-bien-tai-viet-nam-K9Vy8myXLQR)
- SMEs in HCMC remain hesitant to adopt AI because of cost and uncertain efficiency. Many are reluctant to invest heavily without clarity on practical benefits. — [VOV](https://kynguyenvuonminh.vov.vn/en/economy/smes-cautious-about-ai-adoption-amid-efficiency-concerns-post1207935.vov)
- Demand is growing:
  - 44% of small Vietnamese firms named AI their main technology investment in 2024, up from 22% the previous year (CPA Australia survey). — [Tuoi Tre News](https://news.tuoitre.vn/vietnams-small-businesses-lead-asia-pacific-in-ai-investment-survey-10385446.htm)
  - About 170,000 Vietnamese businesses (18%) use AI (AWS study). — [VnEconomy](https://en.vneconomy.vn/nearly-170000-vietnamese-businesses-adopt-ai-aws-study.htm)
- Payments:
  - payOS integrations (Odoo, WordPress/WooCommerce) generate a unique VietQR code per order and confirm payment in real time through a webhook, with a sandbox mode. — [Odoo Apps](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/17.0/payment_payos); [WordPress](https://wordpress.org/plugins/payos/)
  - Vietnamese platforms build subscription engines that send renewal reminders, renew automatically on payment and suspend after a grace period. — [NextCRM](https://nextcrm.vn/nen-tang/thanh-toan)
  - VietQR transfers were described as carrying no transaction fee in an older (around 2022) Sapo post. — [Sapo blog](https://www.sapo.vn/blog/ket-noi-vietqr-pro-tren-sapo-fnb)
- E-invoices:
  - Decree 70/2025/ND-CP took effect 1 June 2025 and amends Decree 123/2020. E-invoices must be in standardized XML, digitally signed, and either cleared with a tax authority code or reported to the tax department on the day of issuance. — [Comarch](https://abc.comarch.com/trade-and-services/data-management/legal-regulation-changes/vietnam-issues-decree-70-2025-introducing-key-reforms-to-electronic-invoicing); [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/us/en/taxnewsflash/news/2025/04/tnf-vietnam-amendments-to-regulations-on-electronic-invoices.html)
  - Decree 70 standardizes issuance deadlines. Some high-volume services may be invoiced by the 7th of the following month. — [Fiscal Requirements](https://www.fiscal-requirements.com/news/3858-vietnams-new-e-invoicing-rules-what-you-need-to-know-starting-june-1-2025)
- VAT:
  - Under VAT Law 48/2024/QH15 (effective 1 July 2025), software products and services are non-VAT items (Clause 21, Article 5). Software companies therefore cannot claim input VAT credits or refunds on domestic sales. — [Rajah & Tann](https://www.rajahtannasia.com/viewpoints/tax-amp-customs-alert-december-2024/); [PwC](https://www.pwc.com/vn/en/publications/2024/241216-new-vat-law.pdf)
  - Caveat: Apolat Legal notes that "software leasing" is not on the Decree 71/2007 software-services list, so tax authorities have treated it as taxable. That view rests on a 2021 HCMC tax letter and predates the new law. SaaS subscription framing may matter. — [Apolat Legal](https://apolatlegal.com/notes-on-determining-the-applicable-tax-rate-software-leasing-services/)

### Inferences
- Suggested packaging for Luvix:
  - Three tiers priced in VND, for example about 990k, 2.5-3M and 5-8M VND/month.
  - A discount of about 15-20% (or "2 months free") for 12-month prepay, to fight SMB churn.
  - A one-off setup or training fee of about 5-30M VND for done-for-you onboarding.
  - Optional usage add-ons (AI credits, messages).
- Collect by bank transfer and VietQR, automated with payOS or similar, issuing a VietQR per invoice. Add MoMo or VNPay later for convenience. B2B buyers expect bank transfer against a red invoice (hóa đơn đỏ).
- Use a licensed e-invoice provider (for example MISA meInvoice, Viettel, VNPT or similar) from day one, because B2B customers need invoices to book the expense. Have an accountant confirm whether Luvix's offering is classed as non-VAT "software services" or as taxable leasing or other IT services.

### Gaps
- payOS, VNPay and MoMo fee schedules and recurring-billing capability were not confirmed.
- No survey data on Vietnamese SME software budgets or willingness to pay for AI tools.
- No Vietnam-specific data on the split between annual prepay and monthly billing.

## 4. Case studies of Vietnamese SaaS companies and how they got early customers and revenue

### Takeaway
Public sources document funding and scale far better than the tactics behind first customers. What is visible:
- Base.vn reached more than 500 SMEs and corporates within about 2 years, with marquee logos.
- KiotViet built a high-volume POS business for micro-merchants with very fast growth.
- Getfly built more than 5,000 SME customers over about a decade with CRM plus Zalo and Facebook integration.
- Sapo and Haravan became platforms with large merchant bases that now offer partner channels.

### Cited Findings
- Base.vn:
  - Founded 2016 by Stanford alumnus Hung Pham, who returned to Vietnam in 2013. Raised seed from VIISA and 500 Startups in 2018, then US$1.3M led by Alpha JWC and Beenext. — [Alpha JWC](https://www.alphajwc.com/en/alpha-jwc-ventures-into-vietnam-with-pre-series-a-funding-for-enterprise-saas-platform-base-vn); [Bizhub](https://bizhub.vietnamnews.vn/basevn-raises-13-million-post301612.html)
  - By the 2018 round, Base had more than 500 SME and corporate customers, including VIB, VPBank, McDonald's and The Coffee House. Later Vietnamese reports cite around 5,000 customers; counts vary by date. — [Bizhub](https://bizhub.vietnamnews.vn/basevn-raises-13-million-post301612.html); [VnEconomy](https://vneconomy.vn/vi-sao-base-ve-chung-nha-voi-fpt-khi-dang-duoc-cac-quy-dau-tu-san.htm)
  - Base later partnered with FPT. — [VnEconomy](https://vneconomy.vn/vi-sao-base-ve-chung-nha-voi-fpt-khi-dang-duoc-cac-quy-dau-tu-san.htm)
- KiotViet (Citigo):
  - Developed in 2014. POS, inventory, CRM and HR software for micro, small and medium businesses.
  - Raised US$2M in 2016 (Seedcom, CVM017), US$6M in 2019 (Jungle Ventures, Traveloka) and US$45M from KKR in 2021.
  - Citigo reported about 250% annual growth since launch. — [DealStreetAsia](https://media.dealstreetasia.com/stories/vietnam-kiotviet-kkr-jungle-258658); [KrASIA](https://amp.kr-asia.com/vietnamese-pos-solution-kiotviet-raises-usd-6-million-from-jungle-ventures-and-traveloka); [Tuoi Tre News](https://tuoitrenews.vn/news/business/20210904/vietnams-startup-kiotviet-raises-45-mn-from-global-investment-firm/62929.html)

### Inferences
- Base's pattern, landing recognizable logos early (banks, F&B chains) and using them as references, is a reusable tactic: offer 3-5 design-partner customers in one vertical a discounted annual deal in exchange for case studies and testimonials.

## 5. Unit math to reach 1 billion VND; churn benchmarks

### Takeaway
1B VND is about US$38-40k (at about 25,000-26,000 VND/USD). The customer-months needed fall steeply as price rises. At 500k VND/month, Luvix needs 2,000 paid customer-months. At 5M VND/month it needs 200. Mixing setup fees and annual prepay makes the goal reachable with roughly 15-90 customers, depending on price point. Plan for 3-7% monthly SMB churn on monthly plans.

### Cited Findings
- SMB SaaS churn is 3-7% monthly. — [Vena](https://www.venasolutions.com/blog/saas-churn-rate)
- Monthly contracts churn 3-5x more than annual contracts. — [Stealth Agents](https://stealthagents.com/research/saas-churn-rate-statistics-2026)
- The Decree 70/2025 threshold of 1B VND annual revenue (cash-register invoices for retail, F&B and similar businesses) is cited as about US$38,462. This gives a reference conversion. — [Fiscal Requirements](https://www.fiscal-requirements.com/news/3858-vietnams-new-e-invoicing-rules-what-you-need-to-know-starting-june-1-2025)
- Benchmark pricing: Getfly at about 521k VND/month for 3 users. — [Viblo](https://viblo.asia/p/9-crm-cho-doanh-nghiep-nho-pho-bien-tai-viet-nam-K9Vy8myXLQR)

### Inferences
The following is my own arithmetic, not sourced.

| Scenario | Price | Revenue needed | Example path |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Low-touch self-serve | 500k VND/mo | 2,000 customer-months | About 83 customers active on average across 24 months. With 5% monthly churn, this means acquiring about 8-10 new customers/month continuously. Hard for an early team. |
| B. SME standard | 1.5M VND/mo (18M/yr) | 667 customer-months | About 56 annual contracts, or an average of about 28 active customers across 24 months. |
| C. Pro or vertical AI | 5M VND/mo (60M/yr) | 200 customer-months | About 17 annual contracts. |
| D. Done-for-you bundle | 20M setup + 4M/mo × 12 = 68M first year | about 15 customers | About 15 customers in year one, or about 8 in year one plus renewals and new customers in year two. |
| E. Mixed (realistic) | 10 × D (680M) + 20 × B annual (360M) | 1.04B | 30 customers total. |

- Plan for 15-25% of revenue going to partner commissions if resellers or agencies are used. Gross collections need to be about 1.2-1.3B VND if commissions are counted against the goal.
- Annual prepaid contracts both pull cash forward and avoid most churn risk within the 12-24 month window.

### Gaps
- No Vietnam-specific churn data for SaaS. The global proxies above may understate churn among micro-SMEs in Vietnam.

## 6. Legal and practical: company setup, contracts, e-invoices, data protection, AI law

### Takeaway
As of October 2026, an AI SaaS selling to Vietnamese businesses must comply with:
- The Personal Data Protection Law (Law 91/2025/QH15, effective 1 Jan 2026) and its implementing Decree 356/2025, which replaces Decree 13/2023.
- The AI Law (Law 134/2025/QH15, effective 1 Mar 2026), which uses three-tier risk classification with transparency and labeling duties.
- E-invoice rules (Decree 123/2020 as amended by Decree 70/2025).
- The new VAT Law (software is non-VAT).

Most typical SME AI tools (chatbots, content, analytics) would likely fall in lower risk tiers, but implementing decrees are still being issued.

### Cited Findings
- PDP Law:
  - Law 91/2025/QH15 passed 26 June 2025 and took effect 1 Jan 2026. It follows the GDPR's architecture with Vietnamese priorities such as national security. — [Baker McKenzie](https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/alerts/2025/10/vietnam-decoding-the-pdp-law-gdpr-inspired-rules-with-local-twist); [PwC](https://www.pwc.com/vn/en/publications/legal-news-brief/250811-personal-data-protection-law.html)
  - It applies to Vietnamese entities and to foreign entities processing Vietnamese citizens' data. — [Hogan Lovells](https://www.hlc.com/en/publications/vietnam-enacts-landmark-law-on-personal-data-protection-stable-standing-with-stricter-compliance)
  - Penalties reach up to 5% of prior-year revenue for cross-border transfer breaches. A draft also listed up to 10x the revenue from illegal data trading and up to VND 3B for other violations; check these against the final text. — [Tilleke & Gibbins](https://www.tilleke.com/insights/vietnams-new-personal-data-protection-law-a-closer-look/2/); [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/vn/en/home/insights/2025/06/vietnam-new-personal-data-protection-law.html)
  - Per KPMG's draft-based summary, startups and small businesses may be exempt from DPIA and DPO obligations for 5 years from the effective date. Confirm this against the final law and Decree 356. — [KPMG](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmg/vn/pdf/2025/06/new-pdp-law-en.pdf)
- Decree 356/2025/ND-CP:
  - Adopted 31 Dec 2025, effective 1 Jan 2026. Implements the PDP Law and replaces Decree 13/2023. — [Digital Policy Alert](https://digitalpolicyalert.org/event/38561-decree-no-3562025n-cp-detailing-certain-articles-and-implementing-measures-of-the-law-on-personal-data-protection-including-operational-licence-requirement-entered-into-force)
  - It moves from principle-based rules to more concrete compliance requirements. — [Rajah & Tann](https://www.rajahtannasia.com/viewpoints/new-decree-implementing-the-law-on-personal-data-protection-passed/)
  - It includes an operational licence requirement, apparently for certain data-processing services. — [Digital Policy Alert](https://digitalpolicyalert.org/event/38560-government-adopted-decree-no-3562025n-cp-detailing-certain-articles-and-implementing-measures-of-the-law-on-personal-data-protection-including-operational-licence-requirement)
  - A comparison with Decree 13 exists. — [LuatVietnam](https://english.luatvietnam.vn/legal-updates/new-features-of-decree-356-2025-compared-with-decree-13-2023-on-personal-data-protection-892-106712-article.html)
- AI Law:
  - Law 134/2025/QH15 was adopted 10 Dec 2025 and took effect 1 Mar 2026. It consolidates the AI provisions previously in the Law on Digital Technology Industry (71/2025). — [Rajah & Tann](https://www.rajahtannasia.com/viewpoints/new-law-on-artificial-intelligence-takes-effect-on-1-march-2026/); [Tilleke & Gibbins](https://www.tilleke.com/insights/a-closer-look-at-vietnams-new-ai-law-what-it-means-for-ai-businesses)
  - It defines "deployer" as an organization using an AI system under its control for professional or commercial purposes. — [Apolat Legal](https://apolatlegal.com/vietnam-artificial-intelligence-law-2025-impacts-and-compliance-roadmap-for-businesses/)
  - It sets three risk tiers. High-risk systems need conformity assessment, either certification by recognized bodies for systems on a Prime Minister list or self-assessment. Medium-risk systems face monitoring and disclosure to users. Low-risk systems mainly face incident monitoring. — [Duane Morris](https://blogs.duanemorris.com/vietnam/2026/03/03/vietnam-the-first-law-on-artificial-intelligence-what-you-must-know/); [Rajah & Tann](https://www.rajahtannasia.com/viewpoints/new-law-on-artificial-intelligence-takes-effect-on-1-march-2026/)
  - Transparency duties: disclose when users interact with AI, and add visible labels on AI content that simulates real people's appearance or voice or recreates real events. — [Duane Morris](https://blogs.duanemorris.com/vietnam/2026/03/03/vietnam-the-first-law-on-artificial-intelligence-what-you-must-know/)
  - Incentives include a regulatory sandbox, R&D and talent tax benefits, SME support and vouchers, and the NATIF fund. Many provisions await implementing decrees. — [Rajah & Tann](https://www.rajahtannasia.com/viewpoints/new-law-on-artificial-intelligence-takes-effect-on-1-march-2026/); [Duane Morris](https://blogs.duanemorris.com/vietnam/2026/03/03/vietnam-the-first-law-on-artificial-intelligence-what-you-must-know/)
- E-invoices and VAT: see Section 3 (Decree 70/2025; VAT Law 48/2024, where software is non-VAT). — [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/us/en/taxnewsflash/news/2025/04/tnf-vietnam-amendments-to-regulations-on-electronic-invoices.html); [Rajah & Tann](https://www.rajahtannasia.com/viewpoints/tax-amp-customs-alert-december-2024/)

### Inferences
- Practical checklist for Luvix:
  1. Operate through a registered Vietnamese company (JSC or LLC) with a software or IT business line, so it can issue VAT e-invoices and sign B2B contracts.
  2. Use a standard Vietnamese-language service contract plus a Data Processing Agreement (DPA). Luvix will usually act as a data processor for customer data.
  3. Publish a privacy notice and handle consent according to the PDP Law.
  4. Document any cross-border transfers, for example calls to foreign LLM APIs. A cross-border transfer impact dossier may be required, and penalties for breaches are high.
  5. Add AI-interaction disclosure in chatbots and labels on synthetic media.
  6. Self-classify each AI feature's risk tier and keep records.
  7. Check eligibility for startup incentives and sandbox programs.
- Calls to foreign LLM providers that send end-customer personal data abroad are probably Luvix's biggest compliance exposure. This is an inference to verify with counsel.

### Gaps
